About The Project

Satyam Surya Manhattan is a premium residential and commercial landmark situated in the heart of Kharghar, Navi Mumbai. Located just a 100-meter walk from the Amandoot Metro Station, this majestic G+37 storey twin-tower project offers breathtaking open views and smart, spacious layouts.

Designed for modern lifestyles, it features a grand entrance lobby, over 1.25 acres of clear title CIDCO tender plot, and world-class amenities. Experience unparalleled connectivity and luxury living at its finest.
