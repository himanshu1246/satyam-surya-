About Satyam Surya Manhattan

Satyam Surya Manhattan is an upcoming premium residential and commercial landmark located in the prime sector of Kharghar, Navi Mumbai. Situated near the Amandoot Metro Station, it offers unparalleled connectivity and an upscale lifestyle. Inspired by modern architectural brilliance, the G+37 storey twin towers feature smart and spacious layouts, breathtaking open views, and world-class amenities for a truly luxurious living experience.
